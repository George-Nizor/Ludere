Ludere handbook

Write at the speed of thought.

Natural flow

Enter moves to the element that logically follows: scene to action, character to dialogue. An empty action line becomes a character cue; an empty dialogue line returns to action.

Automatic structure

INT. and EXT. become scene headings. A short uppercase name becomes a character when committed. Parentheses in dialogue become a parenthetical; CUT TO: becomes a transition.

Fast formatting

Tab and Shift+Tab cycle all eight elements. Ctrl/Cmd+1–8 selects one directly. Ctrl/Cmd+B, I and U apply emphasis without changing screenplay structure.

Local and portable

Your work autosaves on this device. Save a .ludere file for a portable copy, open Final Draft .fdx files, or export .fdx, plain text, printable HTML, and PDF.

Focus sprint

Choose a timer and earlier writing locks. You can only move forward until the sprint ends, even if the app reloads.

Beat board

Switch to Board to plan three acts. Create, reorder, recolor, and move beats between acts; pull scene headings from the current screenplay at any time.
